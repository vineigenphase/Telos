{# Why there is no predicted grade here. Saying it once, plainly, is better than a student wondering why the dashboard treats these differently. #}
These are scored, not graded

TMUA and ESAT report a score from 1.0 to 9.0 against that year's candidates; ENGAA and NSAA reported raw marks. None has an A*–E ladder, so Telos tracks every mark and topic and does not invent a grade. Sit a full mock in Exam Mode for an estimated score on the 1–9 scale.
