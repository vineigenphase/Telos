{# ── What the marking actually does ──────────────────────────────────────── #}

Why bother logging them

A score tells you less than a pattern.

  1. 1
    Enter your answers, not your total. Letters for a multiple-choice paper, marks per question for a written one. It takes about two minutes and it is the difference between knowing you got 14 and knowing which 6 you lost.
  2. 2
    Telos groups the losses by topic. Across every paper you have logged, not just the last one — so a weakness that costs you two marks on every paper shows up as what it is rather than as bad luck four times.
  3. 3
    It tells you what to do next. A short list, ordered by how many marks it is actually worth, rather than a dashboard for you to interpret.
