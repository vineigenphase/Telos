A list of [raw mark, scaled score] pairs, both ascending. Scores between anchors are interpolated. TMUA anchors are on the 40-mark full-sitting basis, so a single 20-question paper is scaled by 40/20 first; ESAT modules are 27 marks and scored separately, as in the real test.

These are estimates. UAT-UK equates each sitting with a Rasch model and anchors the reported scale to that year's cohort — median at 4.5, roughly 10% above 7.0 — but publishes no raw-to-scale table. Every score Telos shows from these must be labelled an estimate.
