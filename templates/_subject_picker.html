{# The subject picker, shared by first-run onboarding and the manage screen. One definition on purpose. The two pages frame the same choice differently — one is a welcome, the other is settings — but the control itself must behave identically, and two copies of a checkbox grid is exactly the kind of thing that drifts until the two disagree about what a subject is. Grouped by subject rather than by board, because that is the order a student thinks in: "Physics — which board am I?" not "OCR A — what do I take?". Level sits between the two. One subject is offered at several levels, and a student takes one of them — so the levels are sub-headings under the subject and the boards are the options under each, rather than a flat list where "AQA A-Level" and "AQA AS-Level" sit side by side and read as near duplicates. #} {% macro subject_picker(qualifications, chosen, levels, chosen_papers) %} {% if levels|length > 1 %}
