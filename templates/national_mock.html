How it runs

Three things happen.

{# Numbered because this genuinely is a sequence: each step only makes sense after the one before it. #}
  1. 1
    You sit it in the window. {{ event.start_local|uk_time }} to {{ event.end_local|uk_time }} on {{ event.start_local|uk_date }}. The clock is server-side and starts when you start — closing the tab does not pause it, exactly as it would not on the day.
  2. 2
    It marks itself immediately. Raw mark, a 1–9 scaled band, and the topics that cost you the most. No waiting for that part.
  3. 3
    The percentile follows. {% if results_due_at %}At {{ event.results_local|uk_when }}{% else %}Once the window shuts{% endif %}, each paper is ranked across everyone who opted in and sat it. If too few people sat a paper for a rank to mean anything, that paper gets no percentile — the mark and the topics still stand. A position among a handful of people is a guess wearing a number.
